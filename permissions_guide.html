App Permission Setup Guide

Maloo Group Recordings Uploader — Enable all required permissions so recordings upload automatically every day without any manual action.

Android 8+ Xiaomi MIUI Samsung One UI Realme / OPPO OnePlus Vivo

All permissions must be enabled for automatic uploads to work

Android and phone manufacturers aggressively restrict background apps to save battery. Skipping any step below may cause recordings to not upload — especially on Xiaomi, Samsung, and Realme devices. Follow every step carefully.

Battery Optimization — Disable
Allow app to run in background without being killed
Required ▼

Autostart / Background Launch
Allow app to start itself in the background after phone restart
Required ▼
Why it's needed When the phone restarts, apps cannot start themselves unless Autostart is enabled. Without this, the daily upload alarm is lost after every reboot and recordings will stop uploading until you manually open the app again.
⚠️ This setting exists only on certain brands. Stock Android (Pixel), OnePlus, and some others do not have a separate Autostart toggle. If you don't see it, you can skip this step.

📱 Xiaomi MIUI

  1. Open Security app
  2. Tap Permissions
  3. Tap Autostart
  4. Find Maloo Recordings → toggle ON
Alt path: Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Maloo Recordings → Other permissions → Autostart → Enable

📱 Samsung One UI

  1. Open Settings → Battery and device care
  2. Tap Battery → App power management
  3. Tap Sleeping apps — if app is listed, swipe to remove it
  4. Also check Deep sleeping apps and remove if listed

📱 Realme / OPPO ColorOS

  1. Open Settings → App Management
  2. Tap Maloo Recordings
  3. Tap Autostart → toggle ON
Also: Phone Manager → App performance → Background freeze → ensure app is NOT frozen.

📱 Vivo FuntouchOS

  1. Open Settings → Apps & notifications
  2. Tap Maloo Recordings
  3. Tap Autostart → toggle ON
  4. Also enable Allow background activity

Storage Access — Select Recordings Folder
Grant the app access to your call recordings folder
Required ▼
Why it's needed Android requires you to manually select the folder where your call recordings are saved. The app cannot access any files until you pick this folder. This is a one-time setup done inside the app.
  1. Open the Maloo Recordings app
  2. On the setup screen, find "Recordings folder" section
  3. Tap Select — your phone's file picker will open
  4. Navigate to the folder where your call recorder app saves recordings
    Common locations: Internal Storage → Call recordings / MIUI / Sounds / CallRecord
  5. Tap Use this folder (or Allow) to confirm access
Not sure where recordings are saved? Open your call recording app → go to its settings → look for "Save location" or "Storage path". Common default folders on Xiaomi: MIUI/sound_recorder/call_rec. On Samsung: Recordings/Call. On stock Android: Call recordings

Background Restriction — Disable
Ensure background jobs are not blocked by the system
Important ▼
Why it's needed If Android has marked this app as "background restricted", all background work (including the daily upload job) is completely blocked. This can happen automatically if the app is not used frequently enough.
  1. Open Settings → Apps
  2. Find and tap Maloo Recordings
  3. Tap Battery
  4. Make sure "Background activity" or "Allow background usage" is enabled
  5. If you see "Background restricted" — tap it and select Unrestricted
How to check: Go to Settings → Apps → Maloo Recordings → Battery. If it says "Restricted", that's the problem. Change to "Unrestricted".
🟡 Additional Settings

Alarms & Reminders
Required on Android 12 for exact daily scheduling
Android 12 only ▼
Why it's needed On Android 12 specifically, apps must have special permission to schedule alarms at an exact time. The app uses this to trigger the daily upload at your configured time (e.g. 7:00 PM). On Android 13 and above, this is automatically granted and you don't need to do anything.
  1. Open Settings → Apps
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) or scroll down to find Special app access
  3. Tap Alarms & reminders
  4. Find Maloo Recordings → toggle to Allow
Note: The app will automatically open this settings page during first-time setup if the permission is missing. Simply tap Allow when that page opens.
🔄
Auto Revoke / Permission Removal — Disable
Prevent Android from removing permissions if app is unused
Recommended ▼
Why it's needed Android 11+ can automatically remove permissions from apps that haven't been opened in a few months. Since this app runs silently in the background and employees rarely open it manually, Android may revoke Call Log permission after 2–3 months of not opening the app — breaking uploads silently.
  1. Open Settings → Apps
  2. Find and tap Maloo Recordings
  3. Scroll down and tap Permissions
  4. Look for "Remove permissions if app is unused" or "Pause app activity if unused"
  5. Toggle it OFF
Can't find this setting? Some devices don't show this toggle. It may appear as: "Delete app if unused", "Revoke permissions if unused", or "App hibernation". Look for any "unused app" related option inside the app's settings page.
😴
App Hibernation — Disable
Android 12+ puts unused apps to sleep, blocking background work
Recommended ▼
Why it's needed Android 12+ can hibernate apps that aren't opened frequently. A hibernated app has all its background jobs stopped and permissions revoked. Since this app runs silently, it's at risk of being hibernated if employees rarely tap on it.
  1. Open Settings → Apps
  2. Find and tap Maloo Recordings
  3. Look for Unused app settings or Hibernate
  4. Disable "Pause app activity if unused" or "Hibernate"
Tip: Opening the app briefly every few weeks also prevents hibernation. But disabling the toggle is the more reliable fix.

Xiaomi MIUI Special Permissions
MIUI has extra restrictions not found on other Android phones
Xiaomi Only ▼
⚠️ Important: Xiaomi MIUI is one of the most restrictive Android skins. Standard battery and permission settings are NOT enough — you must also complete the steps below or uploads will fail silently.

1. Autostart Permission

  1. Open Security app (orange shield icon)
  2. Tap Permissions or Manage permissions
  3. Tap Autostart
  4. Find Maloo Recordings → toggle ON

2. No Background Restrictions

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Manage apps
  2. Find and tap Maloo Recordings
  3. Tap Other permissions
  4. Enable Run in background
  5. Enable Start in background

3. Lock App in Memory

  1. Open the Recent apps screen (square button)
  2. Find Maloo Recordings app card
  3. Tap the lock icon 🔒 on the app card
  4. The app is now locked and won't be killed when clearing recents

4. MIUI Optimization (if issues persist)

  1. Open Settings → Additional settings
  2. Tap Developer options (enable if needed: tap Build number 7 times)
  3. Find MIUI optimization
  4. Toggle it OFF — this gives apps more freedom
Note: Only do this if uploads are still failing after all other steps.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need to keep the app open for uploads to happen? ▼
No. Once set up, the app runs entirely in the background. You can close it completely. The upload runs automatically at your configured daily time (e.g. 7:00 PM). You never need to open the app manually for uploads to work.
Uploads stopped working after a phone restart — what should I do? ▼
This usually means Autostart is not enabled. After a restart, the app needs permission to reschedule its alarm. Enable Autostart (see the Autostart section above for your device). Also open the app once after a restart to verify the alarm is re-armed — then you don't need to open it again.
The app was working before but suddenly stopped uploading — why? ▼
Common causes: (1) Android revoked a permission because the app was unused — open the app and re-grant permissions. (2) Battery optimization was re-applied after a system update — go back to battery settings and set to Unrestricted again. (3) App was hibernated — open the app to wake it up. Your IT admin can also remotely trigger a diagnostic check to see the exact issue.
I don't see a "Manage apps" or "Battery saver" option — where is it? ▼
Settings menus vary between Android versions and phone brands. Try these alternatives: (1) Use the search bar inside Settings and type "battery optimization" or "apps". (2) On Xiaomi, the Security app has many of these settings. (3) On Samsung, look under "Battery and device care". If you're still stuck, ask your IT admin to assist remotely using the health check feature.
Is it safe to grant all these permissions to this app? ▼
Yes. This app is developed and managed by your company (Maloo Group). It only reads call logs and recordings from the folder you selected during setup. It does not access personal messages, contacts, photos, or any other data. All recordings are uploaded exclusively to your company's OneDrive/SharePoint account.
What happens if a recording fails to upload? ▼
The app automatically retries up to 3 times. If all retries fail (e.g. no internet), it logs the failure and tries again at the next scheduled upload. Failed uploads are also reported to your admin through the health check system, so the IT team is notified without you needing to do anything.
